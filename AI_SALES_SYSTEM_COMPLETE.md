## 🚀 **HOW TO USE YOUR CSV LEADS**

### **Step 1: Access the Dashboard**
Navigate to: `https://metisai.tech/ai-sales`

### **Step 2: Import Your CSV**
1. Click "Import CSV" button
2. Select your `Real Estate Commercial ver1.csv` file
3. Configure field mapping:
   - Name → Contact Person
   - Company → Company Name
   - Email → Email Address
   - Phone → Phone Number
   - Industry → Real Estate (auto-set)

### **Step 3: Configure Campaigns**
1. Create a "Real Estate Cold Outreach" campaign
2. Select target criteria (industry: Real Estate)
3. Choose persona (Sarah Chen for enterprise, Alex for general)
4. Set up sequence:
   - Email → Call → LinkedIn → Follow-up

### **Step 4: Launch Automation**
1. Select leads from your imported list
2. Assign to campaign
3. Choose AI persona
4. Start automated outreach

### **Step 5: Monitor Results**
- View real-time call progress
- Track email open rates
- Monitor conversion metrics
- Adjust strategies based on data
